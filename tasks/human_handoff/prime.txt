Route support chats to a human with request_human_handoff(reason), reason one of billing, technical, account, complaint, general. Emit no call when the user states no handoff request.
Examples:
User: I booked train tickets for Saturday. The money left my account but I never got the tickets. I need a person to sort this out.
Call: request_human_handoff({"reason": "billing"})
User: My brother works in customer support.
Call: (none)
User: Transfer me to a person???
Call: request_human_handoff({"reason": "general"})
User: Thanks, that fixed it!
Call: (none)
